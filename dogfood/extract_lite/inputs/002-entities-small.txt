# Entity Map For Northwind Analytics Trial

Mira Chen is the research lead for the Northwind Analytics trial. She owns the evaluation rubric and decides whether the extract artifacts are clear enough for a frontier-model handoff.

Jon Patel is the procurement buyer. He needs a short comparison between the current vendor contract and the proposed LedgerLake subscription before Friday.
